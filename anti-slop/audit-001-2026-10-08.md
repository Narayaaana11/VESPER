## Detailed Findings & Action Plan

### F-01: Em Dash in Landing Page Copy (`R-02` — HIGH)
- **Violation:** R-02 strictly forbids the em dash (`—`) in UI copy and metadata.
- **Location:**
  - `launchpad/src/routes/__root.tsx`: Title tags using `VESPER Desktop — Local-First...`
  - `launchpad/src/routes/index.tsx`: Page title
  - `launchpad/src/components/vesper/Hero.tsx`: Subhead `Hold Alt anywhere on your PC — speak naturally...`
  - `launchpad/src/components/vesper/FaqFeedback.tsx`: FAQ item 4
  - `launchpad/src/components/vesper/DownloadSetup.tsx`: Step 3 setup text
  - `launchpad/src/components/vesper/Roadmap.tsx`: Phase tags `PHASE 1 — TODAY`
  - `launchpad/src/components/vesper/Simulator.tsx`: Filename chips `cursor — auth.middleware.ts`
- **Fix:** Replace all instances of `—` with colons (`:`), commas (`,`), periods (`.`), or slashes (`/`).

### F-02: Em Dash in Desktop Suite App & Components (`R-02` — HIGH)
- **Violation:** Desktop app status lines, settings dropdowns, and notifications contain em dashes.
- **Location:**
  - `Production/src/renderer/src/App.tsx`: `Transcription unclear — please try again.`
  - `Production/src/renderer/src/core/security-gate.ts`: `Autonomous Agent response prepared — review and approve to send.`
  - `Production/src/renderer/src/components/SettingsModal.tsx`: `Any Alt Key (Left or Right Alt) — Recommended`
  - `Production/src/renderer/src/components/ContextBadge.tsx`: `Target Process: App — Title`
  - `Production/src/main/action-executor.ts`: `Context changed — copied to clipboard instead of pasting`
- **Fix:** Replace with colons or clear human punctuation.

### F-03: Fabricated Waitlist Seed Leads (`R-17`, `R-36`, `R-38` — HIGH)
- **Violation:** `INITIAL_SEEDED_LEADS` in `Waitlist.tsx` displays fabricated names and corporate domains (`Dr. Aris Thorne`, `Elena Rostova`, etc.) to fake social proof and activity.
- **Fix:** Remove fabricated personas. Initialize waitlist storage from real client-side submissions in `localStorage`. If no entries exist, display an honest empty queue state (`"No submissions yet. Be the first to join the priority queue."`).

### F-04: Cosmetic Number Spinning Counters in Hero (`R-17`, `R-36` — HIGH)
- **Violation:** `CountUp` to 99 languages and 100% privacy in `Hero.tsx` acts as decorative AI slop.
- **Fix:** Replace generic animated counters with concrete, verifiable engineering metrics: `<180ms Latency` (Groq Whisper-large-v3 turbo), `Direct Foreground Caret Injection` (Win32 SendInput), `Local-First Privacy` (Zero cloud audio storage).

### F-05: Stacked Radial Orbs & Blanket Spotlight Glow (`R-01`, `R-10`, `R-13` — MEDIUM)
- **Violation:** `Hero.tsx` and `Capabilities.tsx` stack 3 layered radial gradient orbs (cyan, emerald, white) with 90 particle canvas objects, and apply intense spotlight glow to every card.
- **Fix:** Restrain glow to single focal point as defined in `DESIGN.md`: deep Obsidian `#0a0a0c` backdrop, crisp 1px `#262632` structural borders, and targeted Emerald accent on active audio recording. Remove unmotivated background radial orbs.

### F-06: Generic AI Clichés & Buzzwords (`R-16` — LOW)
- **Violation:** "Seamless deployment" in `FaqFeedback.tsx`, "Get it done at the speed of thought" in `Hero.tsx`.
- **Fix:** Use precise technical copy: "User-level deployment without UAC elevation" and "Local speech-to-action for Windows power users".

### F-07: Generic CTA & Fake "Popular" Badge in Pricing (`R-15`, `R-05` — LOW)
- **Violation:** `PricingTable.tsx` has `Get Started Free` and default `popular: true` capsule badge.
- **Fix:** Update CTA to `Start Free Locally` and replace unverified `Most Popular` badge with factual architecture tiers (`Local Engine`, `Cloud LPU Engine`, `Enterprise Dedicated`).

### F-08: Trailing Arrows on Buttons (`R-08` — LOW)
- **Violation:** Trailing arrow `→` on `All Releases & Mirrors` link.
- **Fix:** Remove decorative arrow or use semantic external link icon.
