# Role Specification: Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

> **File:** `employees/founder.md`  
> **Role:** Founder & CEO  
> **Department:** Executive Leadership & Strategy  
> **Direct Reports:** Co-Founder, Technical Co-Founder / CTO, Head of Product, Head of Growth  

---

## 🎯 1. Mission & Strategic Vision

The Founder sets the North Star for VESPER: **transforming computing from manual typing and fragmented prompts into zero-friction ambient intelligence.** 

The Founder guarantees that the company builds a product customers genuinely cannot live without, secures sufficient runway, recruits world-class talent, and drives rapid product-market fit (PMF).

### The VESPER Thesis
1. **Typing is an evolutionary bottleneck:** Humans think at ~150-200 wpm, speak at ~130-160 wpm, but type at only 40-70 wpm.
2. **Dictation alone is dead:** Simply spitting speech into a box is a commodity. The real value is **Intent Extraction + Active System Context + Action Execution**.
3. **Privacy & Low Latency Win:** Power users (developers, executives, writers) refuse to send proprietary code or confidential client data through unvetted cloud APIs. A 100% local-first architecture with instant cloud failover is VESPER's unassailable moat.

## 💼 2. Core Responsibilities

### A. Product-Market Fit (PMF) Discovery & Validation
- Conduct 5+ in-depth user discovery calls weekly using the **Mom Test** methodology.
- Personally dogfood every single build throughout an 8-hour workday.
- Track the Sean Ellis PMF Metric: *"How would you feel if you could no longer use VESPER?"* Target: **>40% "Very Disappointed"**.

### B. Company Strategy & Moat Architecture
- Maintain the competitive edge against Cloud Dictation Competitors, Legacy Desktop Tools, macOS Dictation Tools, and native OS solutions.
- Formulate the product pricing strategy (e.g., Free local-tier, $12/mo Pro tier with Groq Whisper-Turbo, Littlebird MCP Enterprise).
- Ensure IP protection around low-latency native Windows input hooks and context synthesis.

### C. Capital Allocation & Runway Management
- Secure angel/seed funding and maintain a minimum of 18–24 months of runway.
- Monitor burn rate, gross margins (minimizing cloud inference costs by routing through local ONNX Whisper where possible).

### D. Evangelism & Culture
- Publicly champion the product on X/Twitter, LinkedIn, and developer podcasts.
- Foster a zero-complacency, customer-obsessed culture focused on speed and high agency.

## 🔍 3. Product Validation Playbook

The Founder validates the product not through vanity metrics, but through brutal user reality:

### 1. The 10-Second Dogfood Test
- **Test:** As soon as a new build is produced, the Founder boots VESPER and dictates a complex Cursor prompt or a messy Slack update while switching between 3 virtual desktops.
- **Pass Criteria:**
  - Zero dropped syllables or key clipping.
  - Latency feels like instant thought-to-screen (<300ms).
  - No focus stealing from the active window.

### 2. The Sean Ellis 40% Benchmark
- Survey cohort every 14 days after 50+ recorded dictations:
  - *Very Disappointed* $\ge 40\%$
  - *Somewhat Disappointed* $< 45\%$
  - *Not Disappointed* $\le 15\%$

### 3. Unit Economics & Retention Validation
- **Cohort Day-1, Day-7, Day-30 Retention:** D1 > 60%, D7 > 35%, D30 > 25%.
- **Daily Active Dictations (DAD):** An active user must invoke VESPER $\ge 15\text{ times/day}$.

## 🤝 5. Cross-Functional Collaboration Matrix

- **With CTO / Technical Co-Founder:** Discusses architectural feasibility, latency budgets, local ONNX vs Cloud APIs, and technical trade-offs.
- **With Co-Founder (COO/CPO):** Synchronizes on hiring, legal compliance, runway, and strategic partnership agreements.
- **With Product Manager:** Translates user feedback into prioritized user stories and ensures PRDs adhere strictly to user needs.
- **With Growth Marketer:** Aligns on positioning narratives, launch sequences, influencer seeding, and conversion funnels.
