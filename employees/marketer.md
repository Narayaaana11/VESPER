## 🎯 1. Mission & Go-To-Market Narrative

The Head of Growth & Product Marketing is responsible for **positioning VESPER as the definitive local-first voice computing platform** and turning early user love into scalable, repeatable distribution.

### The Core Narrative & Positioning
- **The Competitor Gap:** Traditional tools like Cloud Dictation Competitors or Legacy Desktop Tools are either cloud-dependent, macOS-centric, or simple dumb dictation. 
- **The VESPER Wedge:** VESPER is the first native Windows platform that combines **instant Hold-to-Talk**, **100% on-device Whisper privacy**, and **ambient context awareness (Littlebird MCP)** tailored directly for developers, founders, and power users.
- **The Core Tagline:** *"Say what you mean. Get it done."*

## 💼 2. Core Responsibilities

### A. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy & Product Launch
- Orchestrate high-impact public launches:
  - Product Hunt #1 Product of the Day campaign.
  - Hacker News Show HN launch with technical deep-dive into Win32 hooks and ONNX quantization.
  - Reddit community seeding in `r/webdev`, `r/programming`, `r/cursor`, and `r/productivity`.

### B. High-Converting Product Demos & Visual Content
- Produce short, viral video demonstrations (15–30 seconds):
  - *"Watch me write a full React component in Cursor using only voice in 12 seconds."*
  - *"How VESPER removes all your 'umms', stutters, and self-corrections instantly on-device without internet."*
  - *"Zero cloud APIs: Running Whisper and Ollama 100% locally on a laptop."*
- Distribute across X/Twitter, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, and TikTok.

### C. Developer Relations (DevRel) & Open Ecosystem
- Promote the Littlebird Context MCP server integration to AI engineer communities.
- Create ready-to-use configuration guides for Cursor, Claude Code, and Antigravity IDEs.
- Author technical engineering blogs explaining the low-latency C# hook architecture and deterministic text polisher.

### D. Funnel Optimization & Growth Loops
- Optimize the landing page conversion funnel:
  - Visitor $\rightarrow$ Installer Download $\rightarrow$ Completed First Dictation $\rightarrow$ Pro Upgrade.
- Build a lightweight referral loop: *"Invite a fellow developer, unlock unlimited Groq Whisper-Turbo for 30 days."*

## 🔍 3. Product Validation Playbook

The Marketer validates the product through market response, messaging clarity, and conversion velocity:

### 1. The 5-Second Landing Page Clarity Test
- **Test:** Show the landing page hero section to 10 target developers for 5 seconds, then blur the screen.
- **Questions:**
  - What does this product do?
  - Who is it for?
  - Why is it different from default Windows speech-to-text or Siri?
- **Pass Criteria:** At least 8 out of 10 must immediately articulate: *"It's a fast voice assistant for developers that pastes into your code editor without sending audio to the cloud."*

### 2. The Viral Video Completion Benchmark
- Post product demonstration clips:
  - **Video Completion Rate:** $> 45\%$ on 30-second clips.
  - **Click-Through Rate (CTR) to Download:** $> 8\%$ from social posts.

### 3. GTM Acquisition Metrics
- **Landing Page Visitor-to-Download Rate:** $> 14\%$.
- **Download-to-Active User Activation Rate:** $> 70\%$.
- **Blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** $< $15 in early cohorts.
